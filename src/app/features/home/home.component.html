What is The Plastic Route about?

Unlike traditional financial apps, we never ask for your card numbers, passwords, or SMS permissions.

🔒

100% Client-Side Privacy

Zero servers, zero database logins, zero tracking cookies. All portfolio data is stored exclusively in your browser's local IndexedDB.

⚡

Smart Spend Optimization

Instantly routes your spend to the highest yielding card (Amazon, Flipkart, BPCL Fuel, Forex, UPI QR Scans, Dining, Travel, and Wallet loads).

📅

Statement Cycle Leverage

Real-time billing cycle analysis tells you if a transaction today hits your current bill or rolls over into the next cycle for up to 50 interest-free days.

How to Use The App

Get started in three easy steps—no account creation required.

1

Select Purchase Category

Head to the Spend Optimizer, choose your purchase category (e.g., Fuel, Dining, Amazon), and view your primary and backup card recommendations.

2

Check Statement Rollover

Check the Billing Tracker before making major payments to verify if today's transaction safely rolls over into the next statement cycle.

3

Manage Your Portfolio

Visit My Portfolio to view your active card inventory, inspect lounge access thresholds, and check statement generation dates.
